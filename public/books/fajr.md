# Fajr

Fajr is the dawn prayer. It begins at *true dawn* (al-fajr al-sadiq), when light first spreads sideways across the horizon — not the false dawn that is only a vertical glow.

## When the time starts

Astronomically, Fajr is when the sun is **{{fajrAngle}}°** below the horizon. That twilight angle is a setting in this app (often 15°–18°, depending on the community).

## When it ends

The Fajr window ends at **sunrise**, when the sun’s upper limb appears. After that, the next obligatory prayer is Dhuhr.

- Starts: sun at the Fajr twilight angle
- Ends: sunrise
