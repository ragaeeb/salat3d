# Isha

Isha is the night prayer. It begins when red twilight has faded and the sky is dark — the end of astronomical evening twilight for the angle you choose.

## When the time starts

This app treats Isha as the sun **{{ishaAngle}}°** below the horizon (the Isha twilight angle, often 15°–18°). Some methods instead use a fixed number of minutes after Maghrib.

## When it ends

The obligatory Isha time runs until **Fajr**. The middle of the night and the last third of the night (for extra night prayer) are measured from Maghrib to the next Fajr.
