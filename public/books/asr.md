# Asr

Asr is the afternoon prayer. It begins when an object’s shadow has grown to a set length compared with its shadow at noon.

## When the time starts

Two common shadow ratios are used:

- **Shafiʿi / majority:** shadow = noon shadow + the object’s own height
- **Hanafi:** shadow = noon shadow + *twice* the object’s height (later Asr)

This scene uses the majority (Shafiʿi) ratio.

## When it ends

Asr continues until **Maghrib** (sunset). It is disliked to delay it until the sun becomes deep yellow, but the time remains valid until sunset.
