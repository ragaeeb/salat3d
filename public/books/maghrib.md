# Maghrib

Maghrib is the sunset prayer. It begins the moment the sun’s disk disappears below the horizon.

## When the time starts

There is no extra twilight angle. Sunset *is* Maghrib. In the hadith, this is also when the fast of the day is broken — without waiting.

## When it ends

Maghrib lasts until **Isha**, when evening twilight is gone. The window is short compared with the other prayers, so it is prayed soon after sunset.
