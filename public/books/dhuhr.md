# Dhuhr

Dhuhr is the midday prayer. It begins after the sun has passed its highest point — solar noon, or *zawal* — and starts to descend.

## When the time starts

The sign is that shadows, which shrank all morning, begin to lengthen again. This app uses the computed solar transit, then the usual small delay so the prayer does not start *at* the peak.

## When it ends

Dhuhr lasts until **Asr**. The length of that window changes with the season as the sun’s path gets higher or lower.
